# Third-party assets

All bundled assets are public domain (CC0), free to use without attribution.
Credits are listed here anyway, as a courtesy to the creators.

## Models

- **`models/RobotExpressive.glb`** — used for every pedestrian, cop, and the
  player character (rigged, with Idle/Walking/Running/Death animation clips).
  - Model by [Tomás Laulhé](https://www.patreon.com/quaternius) (Quaternius).
  - Modifications (facial morph targets, glTF conversion, material cleanup) by
    [Don McCurdy](https://donmccurdy.com/).
  - License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain).
  - Source: three.js examples
    (`examples/models/gltf/RobotExpressive/`).

Vehicles, buildings, trees, street furniture, the sky, and all textures are
generated procedurally at runtime (no external assets).
